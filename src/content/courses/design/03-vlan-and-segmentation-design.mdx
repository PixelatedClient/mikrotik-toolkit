## Why segment

A segment is a broadcast domain with its own subnet. You split a network to:

- **contain problems**: a loop or a broadcast storm stays in one VLAN,
- **enforce policy**: a firewall sits between segments and decides what may cross,
- **reduce noise**: less broadcast traffic per device,
- **meet rules**: payment or medical devices often must be isolated.

You do not split just because you can. Each segment adds an address block, a gateway and firewall rules to look after.

## A simple method

1. **Group by role and trust.** Staff, guests, servers, cameras, printers, phones, management.
2. **Give each group a VLAN ID and a subnet** using your addressing plan (VLAN 10 staff is 10.x.10.0/24).
3. **Draw the allowed flows** as arrows. Everything without an arrow is denied.
4. **Place the routing.** Small designs route between VLANs on the gateway router. Larger ones use a layer 3 switch or a routed core.
5. **Write the firewall from the arrows.** Allow the arrows, drop the rest.

Example flows for a small office:

| From | To | Allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Staff | Servers | yes (specific ports) |
| Staff | Internet | yes |
| Guest | Internet | yes |
| Guest | Staff, servers | no |
| Cameras | Recorder | yes |
| Cameras | Internet | no |
| Management | everything | yes, from admin PCs only |

## Design choices

**Where to route.** Router on a stick (one trunk to a router with a VLAN interface per VLAN) is simple and fine for small networks. It makes the router link a bottleneck for traffic between VLANs. Beyond a handful of VLANs and heavy inter-VLAN traffic, route on a layer 3 switch.

**Trunks and access ports.** Trunks carry many VLANs between switches and to the router. Access ports carry one VLAN to a device. Keep the list of VLANs on each trunk to what is needed.

**Native or untagged VLAN.** Avoid using VLAN 1 for anything real. Give management its own VLAN and do not leave unused ports in a user VLAN.

**Unused ports.** Put them in an unused VLAN with no gateway, and disable them if you can.

## Management is a segment too

Devices need to be managed even when something else is broken. Put them in a management VLAN, reachable only from admin PCs, and give every device an address there. This is also why VLAN filtering is switched on last when you configure a bridge: the management path must exist before the filter closes.
